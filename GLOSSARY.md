# Market Research

A beginner-oriented context for exploring cryptocurrencies and, in a future expansion, stocks.

## Language

**Asset**:
A cryptocurrency or stock that a user can explore and compare.
_Avoid_: Coin when referring to both cryptocurrencies and stocks

**Research shortlist**:
A set of assets suggested for further investigation based on a user's stated interests and preferences, with explanations of fit and trade-offs. It is not an instruction to buy or an allocation plan.
_Avoid_: Buy list, guaranteed winners

**Investment profile**:
A user's stated time horizon, ability to absorb losses, likely response to a decline, investment experience, and existing investment categories. It provides context for budget-based recommendations without establishing suitability by itself.
_Avoid_: Risk score when referring to the full profile

**Allocation proposal**:
A suggested distribution of the user's entire entered investment budget across recommended assets, expressed as dollar amounts and percentages with reasons and risks. It describes a recommendation rather than owned holdings or an executed purchase.
_Avoid_: Holdings, executed portfolio

**Current value**:
The value a user enters for an existing investment today, used as the starting point for future scenarios. It is distinct from the original purchase cost.
_Avoid_: Cost basis, original investment

**Projected change**:
The estimated percentage change between a future scenario value and its current-value baseline. It is not a record of realized or historical investment performance.
_Avoid_: Realized return, profit to date
