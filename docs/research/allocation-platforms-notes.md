# Wealthfront and StashAway: questionnaire to allocation

Researched 20 October 2026 using current first-party public pages through TinyFish. Documentation research only; no authenticated onboarding or transactions. Do not treat public descriptions as a reproduction of proprietary scoring.

## Wealthfront

- **Inputs:** Its March 2026 explanation identifies age, income/net worth, and personal risk tolerance. Tax level, account type and California residency also affect portfolio selection. [Risk score explainer](https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/ask-wealthfront-risk-score-explainer/)
- **Method:** Questionnaire maps to risk scores from 0.5 to 10 in half-point increments. Each portfolio set has allocations for those scores. The resulting portfolios contain 5–8 asset classes. This is a documented profile-to-portfolio system, rather than a conversational stock-picking assistant. [Risk score explainer](https://www.wealthfront.com/blog/ask-wealthfront-risk-score-explainer/)
- **Inconsistency handling:** Its questionnaire documentation explicitly distinguishes willingness from objective ability to take risk and checks consistency across subjective answers. Inconsistent answers receive a lower score than the simple weighted average. The exact scoring formula is not disclosed. [Questionnaire methodology](https://support.wealthfront.com/hc/en-us/articles/209353586-How-did-you-create-the-questionnaire-to-determine-my-risk-score)
- **Result:** Risk score plus target asset allocation; allocations differ by risk score/tax situation and are maintained through rebalancing. This source describes diversified asset classes rather than personalized coin recommendations. The fetched public quiz did not expose its questions or completed result, so exact radio labels, form validation and missing-input UI remain unverified. [Public quiz](https://www.wealthfront.com/risk-questionnaire)

## StashAway

- **Inputs:** Its account-opening agreement lists financial objectives, risk tolerance, employment status, assets/liabilities/cash flow/income, financial commitments, current investments, whether the amount represents a substantial portion of assets, education and investment/work experience. This is the information the agreement permits collecting, not a verified screen-by-screen questionnaire. [Account-opening agreement, July 2025, section 4](https://www.stashaway.sg/document/AWP_Account_Opening_Document_2025.pdf)
- **Method:** A public explanation says financial assessment produces a recommended StashAway Risk Index (SRI). SRI is a portfolio risk measure based on one-year 99% Value-at-Risk; it is not a guaranteed maximum loss. Lower-risk portfolios contain more protective assets; higher-risk portfolios more growth assets. Exact profile scoring is not public. [SRI explanation](https://www.stashaway.sg/r/which-stashaway-risk-index-should-you-choose)
- **Result:** The current General Investing page presents portfolio type, selectable risk level and USD/SGD view. It describes pre-made portfolios implemented with ETFs, automatic updates, monitoring and rebalancing. The fetched chart remained in a loading state; exact allocations/results were not verified. [General Investing](https://www.stashaway.sg/general-investing)
- **Missing or unsuitable input:** Agreement section 4 says incomplete inputs can limit recommendations to supplied information; further steps or service restrictions may apply where knowledge/experience is insufficient. This establishes that a managed platform does not promise an unconditional personalized result from any input. It does not verify current inline validation behavior. The relevant help-center page fetched only its shell. [Account-opening agreement](https://www.stashaway.sg/document/AWP_Account_Opening_Document_2025.pdf)
- **Historical caveat:** The 2020 SRI article says General Investing did not explicitly ask time horizon and allowed selecting a different risk level. Use it to understand published methodology, not as proof of current onboarding behavior. [SRI explanation](https://www.stashaway.sg/r/which-stashaway-risk-index-should-you-choose)

## Implications for this project (proposed, not competitor facts)

Adopt the pattern **structured form → assessed profile → constrained allocation → explanation**, with no chat or follow-up conversation. Validate required values before submission; show errors beside fields. Use a documented allocation engine for eligibility, exposure limits, portfolio risk and weights; use the specialist AI to research eligible assets and explain verified results. Dollar amounts can scale from validated weights, with cent rounding reconciled to the entered budget.

Do not transplant diversified ETF risk labels or loss assumptions to a crypto-only basket. A conservative preference does not make crypto low risk. The competitors studied do not establish that every profile should receive a fully invested crypto allocation. The project's requested 100% allocation is a product requirement, separate from these platforms' documented methodology; retain an explicit unsupported/error result if data or eligibility checks fail.
